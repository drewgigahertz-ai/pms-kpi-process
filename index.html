Form Builder

Every question becomes a column in Responses and a field you can pivot on. Deadlines are set in Schedule Set Up and accuracy raters in Accuracy Set Up.

Schedule Set Up

Set the deadlines for submissions. One schedule can hold many deadlines, such as KPI Reports due Oct 2, Oct 9 and Oct 16.

People list ()

Assignees, reviewers, ratees and raters are picked from this list. In the real system it comes from the employee directory.

Accuracy Set Up

Pick which forms are rated for accuracy, who is rated (Ratee), on which KPI, and who rates them (Raters). Raters are separate from the schedule's reviewers.

Workflow

  1. Ratee submits the form from Schedules.
  2. Raters get it in their To rate page.
  3. One rater scores accuracy from 0 to 100% and can add a comment.
  4. Accuracy % appears in Responses and in Pivot & Charts.
